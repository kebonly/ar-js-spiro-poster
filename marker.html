Printable AR marker

Print at 100% scale — switch off “fit to page” / “shrink to fit”, or the marker will come out the wrong size and the video will be scaled wrongly against your poster.

Placing it on the poster

  • Keep the white margin around the black square. It is not padding — the detector needs that quiet zone, so do not crop to the square or set the marker on a dark background.
  • Leave roughly two marker-widths of clear space above it. That is where the video appears, and viewers will instinctively compare the two.
  • Use a matte finish if you can. Glossy lamination under conference spotlights produces glare that defeats tracking.
  • Bigger is better for range: an 80 mm square tracks reliably from roughly 0.4–1.2 m. Double the size to roughly double the range.

Sizing check

The video plane is 2.4 marker-widths across, so at 80 mm it renders about 192 mm wide on the poster.
